Building AI-powered software isn't just about calling an API. It's about
designing systems that are reliable, observable, and genuinely useful.

## The Three Layers

When we build AI-powered systems, we think in three layers:

1. **Data layer** — Vector databases, embeddings, and retrieval pipelines
2. **Intelligence layer** — LLM orchestration, prompt engineering, and fallbacks
3. **Application layer** — User interfaces, APIs, and observability

## RAG Done Right

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is the backbone of most production AI
systems. But most implementations get it wrong.

The key is **chunking strategy**. Split your documents too finely and you lose
context. Too coarsely and you waste tokens. We've found that semantic chunking
— splitting by meaning rather than character count — produces the best results.

## Observability

Every AI system needs:

- Token usage tracking
- Latency monitoring
- Quality scoring (human or automated)
- Fallback mechanisms

Without these, you're flying blind.
